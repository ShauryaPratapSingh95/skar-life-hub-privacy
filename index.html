We recommend keeping Windows and your security software up to date. ## 5. Data Deletion You can remove your locally stored SKAR LIFE HUB data by using the application's available deletion features or by removing the application's stored data from your device. Because SKAR LIFE HUB does not transmit user information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of users' personal information. ## 7.
